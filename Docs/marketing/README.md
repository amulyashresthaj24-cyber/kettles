## The product (one line)
**Kettles** — task-linked time tracking that turns focus into a cozy ritual; every minute brews into an accurate, billable ledger, watched by a blue kettle companion. *"Put the kettle on. Get to work."*

## Non-negotiables
- **Blue/neutral only.** No orange/amber, including the mascot. Accent `#0066ff`, kettle `#85c2ff`.
- Fonts: **Urbanist** + **Geist Mono**. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. WCAG AA.
- Use the **real logo** in `assets/logos/` — don't invent one.
