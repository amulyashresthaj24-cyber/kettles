# Row Prompts

Use these prompts with the user's mascot source attached as the identity reference. Each generated row must be a horizontal strip of separated frames on a removable flat background or transparent background, then assembled into the final atlas.

## Shared Style Lock

Preserve the exact mascot identity from the reference: same face, hair or head shape, outfit, palette, glasses/accessories/props, body proportions, outline weight, and readable silhouette. Use pixel-art-adjacent desktop mascot sprites with crisp edges and simple cel shading. Keep the mascot centered and fully visible in every frame.

Forbidden in every row: text, labels, visible grids, frame numbers, UI panels, speech bubbles, detached effects, floating symbols, speed lines, dust, floor shadows, glow, blur, smears, checkerboard backgrounds, or scenery.

## Row 0: idle

Six frames. Calm idle loop with a tiny blink, subtle breathing, or gentle body bob. No waving, walking, working, props changing, or large gestures.

## Row 1: working

Eight frames. Busy focus loop. The mascot should look attentive and concentrated, using small head, hand, or posture changes. Do not show literal running, UI panels, papers, code, or detached tools.

## Row 2: drag_running

Eight frames. The mascot is being carried or dragged, with playful body/limb movement. Keep it self-contained. No dust, floor marks, or speed effects.

## Row 3: waving

Four frames. Friendly wave using only the arm/hand pose. No wave marks, sparkles, or floating punctuation.

## Row 4: jumping

Five frames. Small celebratory jump shown through vertical body pose changes. No shadows, impact effects, or confetti.

## Row 5: failed

Eight frames. Brief disappointed or interrupted reaction. Attached tears, tiny attached stars, or a small smoke puff touching the mascot can be used if they remain opaque and part of the sprite silhouette. No red X, detached smoke, detached drops, or floating symbols.

## Row 6: waiting

Six frames. Paused/waiting loop. Quiet posture, mild blink, or small sway. No sleep bubbles, text, or dramatic emotion.

## Row 7: review

Six frames. Focused checking/reviewing loop through a lean, head tilt, blink, or hand pose. Do not add magnifying glasses, papers, code, or UI unless they already exist in the mascot identity.

## Row 8: running

Six frames. Active in-progress loop for task work. The mascot is busy, but not traveling across the screen. Do not use literal foot-running, sprinting, speed lines, dust, or motion trails.
